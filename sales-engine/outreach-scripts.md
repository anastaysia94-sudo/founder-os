## Follow-up #1 — 1 to 2 business days

Hi — following up on the idea I sent for [BUSINESS].

If the full package isn’t useful yet, I can send the two business-specific samples first so you can judge the quality before deciding. No obligation.

## Follow-up #2 — final cold follow-up

Last note from me — I’ll close the loop unless you’d like the two [BUSINESS]-specific examples.

If you’d rather not receive future outreach, just reply **“no thanks”** and I’ll suppress the contact.

## If they say “yes, send the samples”

Absolutely — here are the two samples.

If the direction fits, I can complete the full Same-Day Customer Growth Pack: customized campaign angle, Google/social posts, FAQs, customer/estimate follow-ups, genuine review-request copy, one promotional email, CTA variants and a 30-day use calendar.

**Starter pack: $150 flat, no subscription.**

## If they ask the price

The starter pack is **$150 flat with no subscription**.

It includes the customized campaign angle, ready-to-publish posts, customer/estimate follow-ups, genuine review-request copy, FAQs, one promotional email, CTA variants and a 30-day use calendar.

I don’t guarantee a specific number of leads or revenue; the deliverable is the completed business-specific marketing and follow-up system.

## If they ask what you need from them

Very little: the exact service/offer you want emphasized, service area, preferred CTA, the phone/booking link you want published, and any claims or wording you want avoided.

I’ll do the writing and organize the finished pack for copy/paste use.

## If they say “not now”

No problem. I’ll leave it here and won’t keep following up.

If [VERIFIED OPPORTUNITY] becomes a priority later, you can reply to this thread and I can pick it back up.

## If they already have marketing help

Makes sense. I’m not trying to replace your agency or existing marketing.

This is a small fixed-scope pack built around one current offer/service opportunity — useful as an extra campaign, follow-up sequence, or customer-education asset.

If that’s not useful, no problem and I’ll close the loop.

## If they ask for a guarantee

No — I don’t guarantee leads, rankings, bookings or revenue.

What I can guarantee is the agreed deliverable: a completed, customized pack based on your approved facts and offer, with no fabricated testimonials or performance claims.

## Close / payment handoff

Great — I’ll send the short intake first so I use only your approved service details, contact information and offer terms.

Once payment is confirmed, I’ll complete the agreed pack and send the finished copy in one handoff.

**Important:** insert only the seller’s real payment instructions. Never invent payment details.

## Delivery follow-up — only after real fulfillment

Hi — checking in on the Growth Pack I delivered. If you’ve started using it, I’d love to know which pieces you deployed first.

If you want, I can also adapt the strongest angle into a second service or seasonal campaign.

## Second-campaign upsell

The first pack focused on [CAMPAIGN 1]. A logical next campaign would be [CAMPAIGN 2] because [VERIFIED REASON].

I can build that as a second fixed-scope campaign without changing your existing system.

## Compliance rules

- Initial contact + maximum two respectful cold follow-ups.
- Stop immediately after an opt-out.
- Do not invent urgency, discounts, contacts, results, clients, testimonials, or revenue.
- Do not guarantee ROI, ranking, lead volume, response rate, or sales.
- Genuine customer review requests only; no review gating, purchasing, or fabrication.
- Reverify current promotions, financing terms, hours and deadlines before using them in copy.
