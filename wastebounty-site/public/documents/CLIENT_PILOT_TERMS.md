# WASTEBounty Client Pilot Terms — Review Draft

**Purpose:** This is a plain-language pilot template for business/legal review before use. It is not legal advice.

## Parties
- Client: [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]
- Service provider: [LEGAL SERVICE PROVIDER / AUTHORIZED ADULT OR COMPANY SIGNATORY]
- Effective date: [DATE]

> **Signatory rule:** The service-provider side must be signed by an authorized adult or legally authorized company representative. Do not use this template to bypass age, capacity, licensing, or local business requirements.

## 1. Pilot scope
WASTEBounty reviews client-authorized records relating to recurring software, AI, cloud, and other digital-vendor spending. The service may identify:
- unused or inactive licenses;
- oversized plans or tiers;
- duplicate capability;
- unused add-ons/modules;
- renewal opportunities;
- other recurring digital-cost reductions supported by evidence.

## 2. Client control
WASTEBounty does **not** cancel subscriptions, modify accounts, negotiate with vendors, change infrastructure, or accept contract terms unless the Client gives separate explicit authorization for that action.

## 3. Baseline
Before savings are counted, the parties agree in writing on the current cost baseline for each opportunity. The baseline should identify the vendor, existing recurring cost, relevant quantity/seat count, renewal term, and evidence source.

## 4. Estimated vs. verified savings
- **Estimated savings** are possible reductions that have not yet happened.
- **Verified savings** are documented reductions from the agreed baseline after an approved action is completed.

Estimated savings are never billable.

## 5. Pilot success fee
Pilot success fee: **20% of mutually verified first-year savings**.

If verified savings are $0, the success fee is $0.

Example:
- Baseline annual cost: $10,000
- Verified new annual cost: $7,000
- Verified first-year savings: $3,000
- WASTEBounty fee at 20%: $600
- Client retains: $2,400 of first-year savings

## 6. Excluded savings
Unless specifically agreed in writing, savings do not include:
- reductions already initiated before the pilot;
- hypothetical or forecast savings;
- disputed or unsupported estimates;
- savings caused by unrelated layoffs, shutdowns, or business closure;
- taxes, accounting adjustments, refunds, or legal claims.

## 7. Evidence
Each proposed opportunity should be recorded in the Savings Register with:
- baseline cost;
- evidence;
- proposed action;
- estimated annual savings;
- approval status;
- verified annual savings;
- fee calculation.

## 8. Data handling
The Client should provide only information it is authorized to share. WASTEBounty does not require banking passwords, payment-card credentials, unrelated customer data, or production write access for the initial review.

## 9. No professional-advice representation
WASTEBounty provides commercial cost-analysis and administrative research. It is not legal, tax, accounting, cybersecurity, investment, procurement-law, or licensed professional advice.

## 10. Payment timing
A success fee becomes invoiceable only after the related savings are mutually verified in writing.

## 11. Termination
Either party may stop the pilot at any time by written notice. Verified savings already completed and documented before termination remain subject to the agreed success-fee calculation.

## 12. Final review
Before external use, this template should be reviewed for the service provider's actual business entity, state/country, taxes, privacy obligations, and contract requirements.
