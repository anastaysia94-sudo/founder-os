# WASTEBounty Partner Follow-Up Playbook

## Initial outreach
Send one individualized message tied to the partner's actual client base or finance role.

## Follow-up timing
- First follow-up: 5–7 business days after initial outreach if no reply.
- Second and final follow-up: 7–10 business days after the first follow-up.
- Then stop unless the prospect re-engages.

## Positive reply
Send:
1. Partner Pilot Methodology
2. Sample Savings Register
3. One-sentence pilot request: one client with meaningful recurring SaaS/AI spend and an authorized vendor/renewal export.

## No reply
Do not resend attachments repeatedly. Use a short plain-text follow-up.

## Bounce
Do not keep retrying the same rejected mailbox.
- verify the address on the official website;
- if still current but rejected, use another official channel such as the company's contact form or a verified staff mailbox;
- record the delivery failure.

## Opt-out / rejection
Stop outreach and record it permanently.

## Duplicate-send prevention
Before sending any attachment package, search the thread for a previous sent message with attachments. If found, do not resend unless explicitly requested.
