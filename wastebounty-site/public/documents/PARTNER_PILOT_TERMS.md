# WASTEBounty Fractional-CFO / Accounting Partner Pilot — Review Draft

**Purpose:** A lightweight collaboration framework for one pilot client. This is a review template, not legal advice.

## Roles
**Partner:** owns the CFO/accounting relationship and determines whether WASTEBounty is appropriate for a client.

**WASTEBounty:** performs the documented software/digital-spend review and prepares the Savings Register.

**Client:** controls all decisions and must authorize any changes.

## Pilot workflow
1. Partner identifies one suitable client.
2. Client authorizes the review and data sharing.
3. WASTEBounty records the baseline.
4. WASTEBounty returns a line-by-line Savings Register.
5. Partner/client approve, reject, or defer each action.
6. Approved actions are carried out only by the authorized party.
7. Actual reductions are documented.
8. Only mutually verified first-year savings enter the success-fee calculation.

## Pilot pricing
Default pilot model:
- $0 upfront success fee.
- 20% of mutually verified first-year savings.
- $0 success fee when verified savings are $0.

Any referral fee, revenue share, or white-label arrangement with the Partner must be separately written and approved before the pilot starts.

## Boundaries
WASTEBounty does not:
- make account changes without authorization;
- accept vendor contract terms for the client;
- give legal, tax, accounting, cybersecurity, or investment advice;
- count hypothetical savings as billable;
- use client information for unrelated marketing.

## Data minimization
Start with the smallest useful dataset: vendor, recurring cost, renewal date, seats/units, active-use count if available, and relevant invoice/order-form evidence.

## Signatory rule
Any binding agreement on the WASTEBounty side must be reviewed and signed by an authorized adult or legally authorized company representative. This document must not be used to bypass age, capacity, licensing, or local business requirements.
