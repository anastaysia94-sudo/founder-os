# WASTEBounty Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**SOP = Standard Operating Procedure: the exact repeatable steps used every time.**

## Gate 0 — Fit check
Accept a pilot only when:
- the client has meaningful recurring software/digital spend;
- the client can legally authorize the data review;
- the client can provide enough evidence to establish baselines;
- someone on the client side can approve actions.

## Gate 1 — Intake
Collect the signed/approved intake plus the minimum dataset.

## Gate 2 — Normalize
Convert records into one vendor list:
Vendor | Category | Current Cost | Renewal | Paid Seats | Active Seats | Owner | Evidence

Do not silently guess missing values. Mark them `Unknown`.

## Gate 3 — Baseline lock
For every opportunity, record the current baseline and evidence before an action is approved.

## Gate 4 — Waste hunt
Check:
1. inactive/dormant seats;
2. former-employee accounts;
3. wrong tier;
4. duplicate tools;
5. unused modules;
6. bundled duplication;
7. near-term renewals;
8. price increases;
9. underused AI subscriptions;
10. optional cloud-cost opportunities.

## Gate 5 — Opportunity QA
Every candidate must include:
- evidence;
- exact recommended action;
- estimated savings method;
- risk/impact note;
- owner;
- confidence;
- renewal/deadline if applicable.

## Gate 6 — Client decision
Status must be one of:
- Candidate
- Needs Evidence
- Approved - Pending
- Rejected
- Verified

Only the client/authorized partner approves an action.

## Gate 7 — Verification
After an approved action:
- obtain invoice/renewal confirmation/new seat quantity or equivalent evidence;
- calculate new annualized cost;
- calculate verified first-year savings;
- record the evidence date.

## Gate 8 — Fee
Success fee = Verified First-Year Savings × agreed rate.

No verified savings = no success fee.

## Gate 9 — Closeout
Deliver:
- final Savings Register;
- summary of implemented reductions;
- unresolved opportunities;
- evidence index;
- invoice for verified savings only;
- client-retained savings summary.

## QA rules
- never count an estimate as revenue;
- never duplicate the same saving across two rows;
- never count a reduction twice across overlapping products;
- never make account changes without authorization;
- never invent usage or contract facts;
- preserve the original evidence reference.
